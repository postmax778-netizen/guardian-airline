01. Before Your Journey

The foundation of a flawless flight begins long before departure. Ensure your documentation and preliminary steps meet our exacting standards.

Passport & Visa

Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifications on our policies and procedures to ensure a perfect journey.

Can I bring my pet on board? keyboard_arrow_down
Guardian Airline accommodates well-behaved pets in the cabin under specific guidelines. Advance notice of 48 hours is required, and strict health documentation must be provided.
What is the cancellation policy? keyboard_arrow_down
Journeys may be canceled or rescheduled without penalty up to 72 hours prior to departure. Modifications within 72 hours are subject to a concierge fee.
Are dietary restrictions accommodated? keyboard_arrow_down
Absolutely. La Table du Griffon crafts bespoke menus for every flight. Please inform your concierge of any allergies or preferences at least 24 hours prior to your journey.
